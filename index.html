How to Refer

1

Use the triage tools above

For AT-HM clients, use the Decision Tool or Flowchart to confirm whether a Kindly referral is appropriate.

2

Submit via the Kindly website

All referrals are submitted through the referral form at kindlyhealthcare.com.au — no email required.

3

Kindly contacts the client

Our team picks up the referral and schedules an assessment. You receive a confirmation and outcome report.

4

AT-HM: approve and done

For AT-HM clients, Kindly handles sourcing, quoting, and supplier liaison. You simply approve the quote.
